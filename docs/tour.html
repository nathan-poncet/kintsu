The tour

One terminal, one story. Pick a chapter and it plays from there; each one says what is happening, who is doing the work and where. Click a word in the bubble, or focus the terminal and press Tab, ^K, w, f, a, i, p, Esc, to control it yourself. Space pauses.

Scripted scenes of the designed behaviour; Kintsu is pre-alpha and real recordings replace them the day v0.1 runs. Nothing here runs a command, and nothing will in the real thing either.
