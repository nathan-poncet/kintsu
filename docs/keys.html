Keys and commands

Every action has a key, a clickable word and a command. The single letters work once the panel is open.

wherekeydoes
at the prompt, empty lineTab or →accept the ghost-text fix (zsh, fish)
at the prompt^Kopen the last bubble as a panel, or close it
at the promptclick a wordopen the panel on that action (terminals that render OSC 8 links)
panelw f a i pWhy · Fix · Agent · Ignore · Privacy
panel⏎insert the focused suggestion in your line and close the panel; you press Enter again to run
panelccopy the focused suggestion
panelTab · ↑ ↓ · wheelmove focus · scroll
panelEsc or qclose the panel
kintsu why · fix · agent · privacy · ignoreact on the last failure; why answers above your prompt while you keep working
kintsu ignore --command|--dir|--session|--alwaysstop bubbles for that command, directory or session, or permanently
kintsu mute 1hno bubbles for one hour
kintsu setup · doctor · default-config · config pathconfigure in three questions; check the hook, the models and the keys; print the default file; show where the files are
kintsu daemon status · stopthe resident process; the hooks start it on their own, messages from your model arrive through it
kintsu service install · uninstallkeep the daemon alive across logins (launchd, systemd) and register the kintsu:// scheme so the bubble's words are clickable
kintsu open kintsu://act?case=…&do=…what the desktop runs when you click a word; the answer lands in the shell the failure happened in
kintsu init zsh|bash|fishthe hook, to eval or source
KINTSU_DISABLE=1disables the hook in that shell
