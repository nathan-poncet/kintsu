Keys and commands

Every action, three ways.

A key, a word you can click, and a command. Nothing steals a keystroke from your prompt; the single letters only work once the panel is open.

wherekeydoes
at the prompt, empty lineTab or →accept the ghost-text fix (zsh, fish)
at the prompt^Kunfold the last bubble into the panel, or fold it
at the promptclick a wordopen the panel on that action (terminals that render OSC 8 links)
